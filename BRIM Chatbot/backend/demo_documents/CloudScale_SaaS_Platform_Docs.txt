# CloudScale SaaS Analytics & Automation Platform Documentation

## Product Overview
CloudScale is an enterprise cloud analytics, workflow automation, and observability platform that helps engineering and product teams monitor application performance, automate customer journeys, and aggregate event streams in real time.

## Pricing Plans & Feature Matrix
1. **Starter Plan ($49 / month)**
   - Up to 500,000 monthly events.
   - 3 team member seats.
   - Standard email support with 24-hour SLA.
   - 14-day data retention.

2. **Growth Plan ($199 / month)**
   - Up to 5,000,000 monthly events.
   - 15 team member seats.
   - Automated funnel analytics, anomaly detection alerts, and Slack integration.
   - Priority chat support with 4-hour SLA.
   - 90-day data retention.

3. **Enterprise Plan (Custom Pricing — starting at $899 / month)**
   - Unlimited event volume with dedicated throughput.
   - SAML 2.0 / Okta SSO, custom role-based access control (RBAC).
   - Dedicated Solutions Architect and 99.99% uptime guarantee with financial credits.
   - Custom data retention policies (up to 3 years) and on-premise VPC peering.

## Technical Specifications & Integrations
- **API Rate Limits**: 2,000 requests per minute per API token on Growth, 10,000 requests per minute on Enterprise.
- **SDK Support**: Python, Node.js, Go, Java, React, iOS, and Android.
- **Security & Compliance**: SOC 2 Type II certified, GDPR compliant, HIPAA BAA available upon request, data encrypted at rest with AES-256 and in transit with TLS 1.3.
- **Support Contact**: Enterprise support desk available at support@cloudscale.io or via portal ticket.
